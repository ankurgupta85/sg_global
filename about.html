About

SG Global PTE Ltd is an industry leader in the trading of used or new machines; even complete Plant & Machinery and various types of raw material/tooling for the past 30 years. We also deal in Fastener tooling products like Thread Rolling Dies, Punch, Heading Dies, Carbide Pallets, Taps etc, in addition we have Tungsten Carbide products, Inserts, Steel, Copper Wire, Copper Tubes, Recycled Plastic, Screws, Washers and several others. Our company caters to all kinds of machinery requirements for various industries like steel, fastener, engineering, plastic, automobile, power plants, paper etc.

Primarily based in Singapore, we have carved ourselves a niche in this business by providing a wide spectrum of machines that range from different models, makes and types, new, used and refurbished. We also deliver machinery Ex-factory, FOB port or CNF. Our years of experience in this trade has enabled us to deliver consistent performance as we regularly supply Industrial Machinery to customers in Asia and Worldwide. To facilitate our customers we have a division of freight forwarding for movement of goods from any part of the world and have a clearing agency division in India for any import or export to or from India.
